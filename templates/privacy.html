Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address for account creation and the expense data you enter into the application (amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions). We also collect basic technical data such as IP addresses and browser types to ensure the service functions correctly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Spendly experience. This includes managing your account, calculating your spending summaries, and providing the analytics tools within the app. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement encryption and other security protocols to prevent unauthorized access. You have the right to request a copy of your data or request that your account and all associated data be deleted at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your financial data with third-party services. Any third-party tools used for hosting or basic analytics operate under strict privacy agreements and do not have access to your raw expense entries.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our privacy officer at privacy@spendly.example

Your privacy is our priority. Thank you for trusting Spendly with your financial tracking.
